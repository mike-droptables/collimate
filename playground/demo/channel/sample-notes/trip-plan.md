# Trip plan

Three days in Lisbon in late October, staying near Alfama.

## Day 1

- Arrive mid-morning, drop the bags at the flat
- Walk down to the river and along to Praça do Comércio
- Dinner somewhere small in Alfama

## Day 2

- Tram 28 early, before the queues
- Belém: the monastery, then pastries
- Sunset from the Santa Catarina lookout

## Day 3

- Slow morning at a café
- Time Out Market for lunch, then the airport
